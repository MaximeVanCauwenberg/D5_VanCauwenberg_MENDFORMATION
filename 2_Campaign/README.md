# MENDFORMATION campaign

Three campaign phases share the same geometric, brutalist visual language.

| Phase | Channel | Asset and format | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teaser | Sponsored website banner | `01_teaser_website_300x250.png` · 300 × 250 px | SOMETHING IS BEING REBUILT. |
| Launch | Campus poster | `02_launch_campus_poster_A3.pdf` · A3 | THE CITY REBUILDS. |
| During | Festival signage | `03_during_festival_sign_A2.pdf` · A2 | DON'T JUST LOOK. CREATE. |
| Supplementary | Digital bus-stop screen | `04_bus_stop_digital_screen_1080x1920.png` · 1080 × 1920 px | TRASH? THINK AGAIN. |

Editable SVG files preserve the layout geometry. `Campaign_assets_in_context.pdf` shows the designs in their proposed settings.

**Core line:** MAKE MORE. WASTE LESS.
